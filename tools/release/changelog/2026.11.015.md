## What changed since 2026.11.014

### Chat and sound

- **Enter opens the chat again**, in games and in replays. Since the new window layer, one press of Enter opened the chat and closed it again straight away, so nobody could write in a game (#488).
- **3D sound on 5.1 and 7.1 setups sounds as it did in the original game.** The original game played every sound at full volume on surround outputs it didn't recognise; this engine faded distant sounds there, so zoomed-out fights got much quieter than players were used to. It now plays them as the original did, and also sends sounds to the subwoofer as the original did. `snd_RetailSpatial 0` keeps the distance fade on every output (#488).

### Multiplayer and replays

- **Games with mods that turn off a bone and the bones under it no longer desync**, such as Random Events' Lattice units (#489, by lL1l1).
- **Restarting during a replay no longer crashes the game** or turns the replay into a live game. As in the original game, the quit menu no longer offers "Restart Session" in replays and multiplayer games (#490, by lL1l1).

### Graphics

- UEF shields are no longer drawn black on Direct3D 11, and a changed graphics fidelity setting now applies to terrain and water right away (#425, by Marlo).
- Bloom glow sits on the object that glows again on Direct3D 11, Direct3D 12 and Vulkan, instead of drifting down and to the right (#485, by lL1l1).
- Dark half-circles and blobs next to units' legs, which looked like extra shadows on the ground, are gone (#484).

### Window and interface

- Resizing the game window after leaving full screen works again, and full screen opens on the monitor the window is on (#481, by GodFuper).
- A rare crash while the game was loading, when a window took focus at the wrong moment, is fixed.
- The log window stays above the game, has no taskbar or Alt+Tab entry of its own, and has only a close button (#487, by lL1l1).
- Typing a console variable's name in the console now shows its value there (#486, by lL1l1).

### For testers and developers

- `snd_RetailSpatial` (on) and `/nosndretailspatial`, in `docs/SWITCHES.md`. The game log now says how many channels the sound output has and what the Windows speaker setting gives (`SND: output has N channels ..., the speaker setting M`), which tells which case a sound report is.
- Messages from the game to the FAF client wait for the client's first connection instead of being dropped, with tests for the order the Java client connects in (#483).

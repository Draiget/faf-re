# Legal

faf-re is an independent reverse-engineering, preservation, and interoperability project based on the Moho engine used by Supreme Commander: Forged Alliance.

The project contains reconstructed engine implementations developed through analysis of the original software, alongside independently developed tools and improvements.

Supreme Commander, Forged Alliance, and the original Moho engine remain the property of their respective rights holders. No ownership of the original software or its intellectual property is claimed.

This repository contains no game data or original game binaries. Running the engine requires an installed copy of Forged Alliance.

No repository-wide open-source license is currently granted. Any future licensing of independently authored components will be identified separately and will not grant rights to third-party intellectual property.

Third-party libraries keep their own licenses, found in their folders and listed in the README. Nothing here limits the rights those licenses give.
